# TECHNOVA RETAIL - RETURN, REFUND & WARRANTY POLICY

## SECTION 1: GENERAL RETURN TIMEFRAMES
- **Standard Return Window**: Most unopened, brand-new merchandise in original packaging can be returned within 30 calendar days of the purchase date for a full refund to the original payment method.
- **Proof of Purchase**: A valid store receipt, digital order confirmation, or order ID is required for all returns and exchanges.

## SECTION 2: CATEGORY-SPECIFIC RETURN POLICIES
- **Electronics & Computer Hardware**: 
  - Return window is strictly **30 calendar days** from the date of purchase.
  - Opened items in like-new condition with all original accessories, cables, manuals, and packaging are subject to a **15% restocking fee**.
  - Defective items may be exchanged for the exact same model within 30 days without any fee.
- **Apparel, Footwear & Accessories**: 
  - Return window is **14 calendar days** from the date of purchase.
  - Items must be unworn, unwashed, with original tags intact and attached.
- **Smart Home & Wearable Devices**:
  - Return window is **30 calendar days**. Must be factory reset before returning.
- **Jewelry, Necklaces & Fashion Accessories**:
  - Return window is **30 calendar days** from purchase date.
  - Must be in unworn condition in original packaging/box with proof of purchase.
  - Covered by a **90-day workmanship warranty** against chain breakage, clasp defects, or tarnishing.
- **Clearance & Final Sale Items**:
  - Any item marked as "Clearance", "Open-Box Final", or purchased at discounts of 50% or higher is **Final Sale** and is **Non-Returnable / Non-Refundable** (0 days).
- **Consumables & Personal Care**:
  - Batteries, software licenses, ear tips, and grooming products are non-returnable once opened.

## SECTION 3: WARRANTY COVERAGE
- **TechNova 1-Year Limited Hardware Warranty**:
  - All brand-new Electronics, Smart Home, and Wearable devices include a **365-day manufacturer/store limited warranty** from the purchase date covering hardware defects and component failures.
  - Warranty does not cover accidental physical damage, water damage, unauthorized repairs, or normal wear and tear.
- **Apparel & Accessories**:
  - Covered by a **60-day warranty** against manufacturing defects (stitching, zippers, seam failure).

## SECTION 4: RETURN SHIPPING & REFUND PROCESSING
- **Online Returns**: Customers can initiate returns through our portal or customer service agent. TechNova provides a pre-paid return shipping label for defective items. For remorse returns, a $5.99 return shipping deduction applies.
- **In-Store Returns**: Returns brought to any physical TechNova store are free of return shipping fees.
- **Refund Timeline**: Refunds are credited back to the original payment method within 3-5 business days after the returned product is inspected at our warehouse.

## SECTION 5: ORDER TRACKING & FULFILLMENT
- Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days. Express shipping takes 1-2 business days.
- Tracking numbers are updated in our live order tracking system within 12 hours of dispatch.
- Orders in "Processing" status can be cancelled before dispatch. Orders in "In Transit" or "Delivered" cannot be cancelled and must proceed through the standard return workflow.
